
# My Favorite Series

## The Boys

**The Boys** is one of my favorite series because it offers a unique take on superheroes, showing what happens when people with superpowers become corrupt and abuse their influence.

### Why I Like It
- The storyline is interesting and unpredictable.
- The characters have unique personalities and backgrounds.
- It shows a darker and more realistic side of superheroes.
- The action scenes and plot twists make it entertaining.

### My Favorite Character
**Billy Butcher** – I like him because of his fearless attitude, determination, and willingness to fight against corrupt superheroes.

### Rating
⭐ 9/10

> "Never meet your heroes."
